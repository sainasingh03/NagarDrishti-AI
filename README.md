## 🌍 About NagarDrishti AI

Road hazards are more than infrastructure problems. They can affect
daily commutes, vehicle safety, accessibility, and emergency travel.

**NagarDrishti AI** is a civic-tech project designed to explore how
artificial intelligence and location-aware data can help identify,
visualize, and monitor road hazards.

The project focuses on three important categories:

- 🕳️ **Potholes** — identify potential road-surface damage.
- 🛣️ **Road Cracks** — help recognise visible cracks in road surfaces.
- 🌧️ **Waterlogging** — identify potential water accumulation on roads.

The long-term vision is to connect road-condition observations with
geospatial mapping, hazard verification, safer-route awareness, and
infrastructure follow-up.

> **Our mission:** Turn road-condition observations into meaningful
> information that supports safer journeys and better-informed
> infrastructure decisions.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Road damage and waterlogging can create risks for drivers, pedestrians,
cyclists, and other road users.

However, road-condition information can be fragmented across photographs,
messages, local observations, and separate reporting channels.

This creates several challenges:

- Road hazards may be difficult to locate quickly.
- Visual evidence may not be organised into structured records.
- People may not know whether a previously reported hazard still exists.
- Unverified information can become outdated.
- Infrastructure teams need reliable evidence to prioritise inspections.

NagarDrishti AI explores a technology-driven workflow to help address
these challenges.
